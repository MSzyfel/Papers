\textbf{Key Findings:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Greedy algorithm offers excellent runtime with good approximation
    \item PTAS provides better quality but at higher computational cost
    \item FPTAS strikes best balance for fine approximations
    \item Choice of algorithm depends on time-quality requirements
\end{itemize}
