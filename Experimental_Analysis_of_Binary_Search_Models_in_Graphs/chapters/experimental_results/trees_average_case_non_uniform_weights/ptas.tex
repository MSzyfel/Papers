\textbf{Observations:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item PTAS achieves guaranteed approximation bounds
    \item Runtime increases as $\epsilon$ decreases (better approximation)
    \item Trade-off between solution quality and computation time
    \item Practical approximation ratios often better than theoretical guarantee
\end{itemize}
