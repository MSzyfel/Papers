\subsection{Large Diameter Trees ($T_{\bar{D}} || V || \COST$)}
\label{sec:large_diameter}

Large diameter trees are constructed to guarantee diameter $\geq D$. A path of length $D$ is constructed from the root, and remaining vertices are attached randomly to vertices along this path.
