\textbf{Observations:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Lower degree bounds lead to deeper decision trees
    \item As $\Delta$ increases, average depth decreases and approaches random tree performance
    \item Weight distribution has less impact on bounded degree trees than on random trees
    \item Runtime remains efficient across all degree bounds and weight distributions
    \item Degree constraint forces more balanced structures, reducing variance
\end{itemize}
