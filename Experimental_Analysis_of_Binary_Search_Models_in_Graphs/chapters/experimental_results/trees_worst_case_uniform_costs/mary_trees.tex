\subsection{Full m-ary Trees ($T_m || V || \COST$)}
\label{sec:mary_trees}

Full m-ary trees are trees where every internal node has exactly $m$ children. These trees provide balanced structures with predictable properties. Our experiments use $m=3$ (ternary trees).
