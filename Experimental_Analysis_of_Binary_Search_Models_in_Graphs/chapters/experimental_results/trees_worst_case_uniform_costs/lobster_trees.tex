\subsection{Lobster Trees ($T_{lob} || V || \COST$)}
\label{sec:lobster_trees}

Lobster trees are a generalization of caterpillar trees where some leaves of the caterpillar's spine have their own leaves attached, creating a three-level hierarchical structure.
