\subsection{Large Degree Trees ($T_{\delta} || V || \COST$)}
\label{sec:large_degree}

Large degree trees are constructed to guarantee at least one vertex with degree $\geq \delta$. A root vertex is connected to exactly $\delta$ other vertices, and remaining vertices are attached randomly.
