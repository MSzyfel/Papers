\subsection{Preferential Attachment Trees ($T_{PA} || V || \COST$)}
\label{sec:preferential_trees}

Trees grown using the Barabási-Albert preferential attachment model where each new vertex attaches to existing vertices with probability proportional to their current degree, creating scale-free networks.
